Mitral Valve Segmentation

Segmenting the mitral valve from ultrasound videos with a U-Net

PyTorch U-Net Medical Imaging Computer Vision

Overview

Final project for the "Advanced Machine Learning" course (Prof. Joachim M. Buhmann, ETH Zurich). The goal was to automatically segment the mitral valve from apical four-chamber (A4C) echocardiography videos using a U-Net.

Approach

Three U-Net variants were trained: one for valve segmentation, one for bounding-box localisation, and a combined multi-task model that does both with shared features. Frames were resized to 512×512 and augmented ~45× with rotations, intensity scaling and elastic deformation. Training used an IoU-based loss (with a median variant for robustness to outliers) and the Adam optimiser.
