Neural Operators for PDEs

Solving and discovering partial differential equations with neural networks

PyTorch Neural Operators PDE Discovery Scientific ML

Overview

Final project for the "Artificial Intelligence for Science and Engineering" (AISE) course at ETH Zurich. It has three parts: solving the wave equation with Fourier Neural Operators, discovering unknown PDE terms from data, and modelling phase transitions with the Allen-Cahn equation.

Course

AI for Science and Engineering

Institution

ETH Zurich

Year

2024/2025

The three parts

1

Fourier Neural Operators

FNOs for the 1D wave equation, using spectral convolutions in Fourier space.

2

PDE Discovery

Identifying unknown governing equations directly from observational data.

3

Phase Field Dynamics

Solving the Allen-Cahn equation for phase transitions and interface dynamics.

Part 1 — Fourier Neural Operators

FNOs perform their convolutions in Fourier space, which suits the periodic structure of the wave equation. The model was trained across several spatial resolutions (32–128 grid points) and tested on unseen resolutions and time horizons.

FNO predictions for the 1D wave equation across resolutions and time steps.

Part 2 — PDE Discovery

A gradient-based method identifies which differential terms make up an unknown PDE from data. The terms it can recover include:

Linear Terms

u v

Diffusion Terms

∂²u/∂x² ∂²v/∂y²

Advection Terms

∂u/∂x ∂v/∂y

Nonlinear Terms

u² v² u·∂u/∂x

Coupled Terms

A²u A²v where A² = u² + v²

Gradient computation used to recover the differential terms of an unknown PDE.

Part 3 — Allen-Cahn Phase Field

Allen-Cahn Equation

∂u/∂t = ε²∇²u + u - u³

ε controls the interface width; u is the phase field variable.

The interface width ε spans two orders of magnitude, from sharp to diffuse. Curriculum learning — training on easier (larger ε) cases first — let a single network handle the full range and extrapolate beyond the training time horizon.

Phase field evolution for the Allen-Cahn equation (ε = 0.25), on an out-of-distribution case.
