Olga I. Vinogradova, Elena F. Silkina · arXiv · 2 Oct · `struct-pred`

> Derives closed-form surface charge-potential relations for charged spheres across the full curvature range, replacing numerical Poisson-Boltzmann solving with an exact dual-asymptotic formula that spans weakly curved to highly curved regimes.

[url](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03613v1) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.03613v1)

Connection: An assembly oracle needs a cheap way to score whether a designed interface's electrostatics are compatible at the specific local curvature it sits on in an icosahedral shell, since high- and low-curvature subunits see very different charge-potential behavior. This paper's exact, curvature-indexed formula is a physical scoring term that could substitute for per-design Poisson-Boltzmann runs, giving assembly propensity without needing experimental positives to train against.
