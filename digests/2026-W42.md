### [track] How Fluctuations Control Chiral Symmetry Breaking in Reacting Mixtures of Chiral Molecules
Leonardo Silva-Dias, Christoph A. Weber · arXiv · 9 Oct · `ml-method`

> This models chiral symmetry breaking in small reacting populations as a stochastic, fluctuation-driven selection problem rather than a thermodynamic one, deriving how finite-population noise decides which of several equivalent states a system commits to and how long that commitment takes.

[url](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13523v1) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13523v1)

Connection: Symmetry-native tied-ASU diffusion and quasi-equivalence both assume a subunit's commitment to one of several degenerate symmetric assembly outcomes is governed by free energy, but this paper's analytical treatment of finite-population fluctuations deciding between mirror-symmetric outcomes suggests assembly-slot or conformer choice in quasi-equivalent capsids may instead be a kinetic, fluctuation-dominated branching process. The same bias-dependent selection-time formalism could motivate building design-quality oracles around stochastic commitment probabilities rather than static thermodynamic scores.

Confidence: medium
