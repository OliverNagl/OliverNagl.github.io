Indira Barros, …, Indrani Chakraborty · arXiv · 23 Oct · `ml-method`

> Patchy hematite-TPM colloids with directional magnetic patches switch between distinct multi-particle architectures purely by tuning external field frequency/amplitude, with no change to particle chemistry.

[url](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32872v1) · [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32872v1)

Connection: This bears on quasi-equivalence: it shows that identical patch-interaction rules can be steered into multiple distinct assembly outcomes by an external control parameter rather than by intrinsic subunit flexibility, which is a physical model for how one designed sequence might be pushed into different backbone conformations depending on assembly slot. It also reframes assembly as kinetically gated by field (control-parameter) history rather than as a single thermodynamic minimum, which is the kind of kinetic-trap framing the field underweights relative to equilibrium design criteria.

Confidence: low
