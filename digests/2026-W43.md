### [track] Kinetic-driven microphase separation and vitrification of crowded hydrogels with associative bonds.
Shi D, …, Gong JP · Science advances · 16 Oct · `ml-method`

> A crowded associative-bond hydrogel resolves into either homogeneous vitrification or confined microphase separation depending purely on the competing timescales of elastic relaxation versus diffusive demixing along the approach trajectory, not on which state is the free-energy minimum.

[url](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42886778/) · [doi](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aed8808)

Connection: This is a concrete kinetic-trap mechanism for the assembly-oracle problem: a subunit thermodynamically capable of closing into a shell can still arrest into an off-pathway aggregate if local bond-formation outruns global diffusive reorganization. An oracle trained only on structural or thermodynamic features of the target state, with no path-rate term, would systematically miss exactly this failure mode.

Confidence: medium
